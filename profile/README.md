## About LUNEVON

**LUNEVON** is an independent software and technology brand focused on practical system tools, Android applications and engineering research.

**LUNEVON LABS** is the engineering space behind the brand — where products are designed, validated and documented.

Our work is centered around a simple idea:

> **Turn complex device and system data into clear, useful information.**

## Engineering principles

LUNEVON products are built around a small set of engineering principles:

- **Local-first processing** where practical.
- **Minimal permissions** and explicit user control.
- **Clear technical information** instead of opaque indicators.
- **Consistent product design** across the LUNEVON ecosystem.
- **Reproducible validation** before release.
- **No unnecessary platform complexity.**
